Privacy policy

AppleHealth4Me reads Apple Health data on the user's iPhone, only after the user allows it in iOS.

The data is written directly from the iPhone to the user's own Google account: a Google Sheet and files in a Google Drive folder the user chooses. Google sign-in is used only for this.

The app has no server. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

The user can stop at any time: disconnect Google in the app, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the files in Google Drive.
